📲 Install App & Mobile Camera Access

This app runs 100% offline with zero backend setup. You can use it on your computer or connect your smartphone on the same Wi-Fi!

📱 Open & Use on your Phone (Wi-Fi)
  1. Run Launch-App.bat on your PC.
  2. Look at the black console window to see your computer's local Wi-Fi address (e.g., http://192.168.1.X:8080).
  3. Open that address in your phone's browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari).
  4. Tap "📸 Camera / Gallery" to snap pictures of physical products directly with your phone's camera!
🤖 Android PWA Installation
  1. Open this page in Google Chrome or Edge.
  2. Tap the "📲 Download App" button above, or open the 3 dots (⋮) menu.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Launch it like a native app anytime!
🍏 iPhone & iPad (iOS)
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with up arrow).
  3. Select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner.
💻 Windows PC / Mac
  1. In Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge:
  2. Click the Install icon (⊕) on the right side of the address bar.
  3. Enjoy full-screen offline inventory tracking!
