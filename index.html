📲 Download & Install Choy Apparel App

Install Choy Apparel directly onto your phone, tablet, or PC. It operates 100% offline with zero app store delays!

🤖 Android (Chrome & Edge)
  1. Tap the "📲 Download App" button above, or open the 3-dots menu (⋮).
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. The Choy Apparel icon will be placed directly onto your home screen!
🍏 iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with up-arrow at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner. It runs in its own window without browser bars!
💻 Windows PC / Mac
  1. In Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge:
  2. Click the Install icon (⊕) on the right side of your address bar.
  3. Or double-click Create-Desktop-App-Shortcut.bat in the app folder to generate a 1-click desktop icon!
📶 How to Connect Phone over Wi-Fi
  1. Run Launch-App.bat on your PC.
  2. Look at the black console window for your computer's local Wi-Fi IP address (e.g., http://192.168.1.X:8080).
  3. Open that address on your phone to shoot product photos with your smartphone camera!
